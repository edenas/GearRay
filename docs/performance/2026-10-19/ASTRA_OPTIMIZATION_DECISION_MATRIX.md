## Selection rule

First establish correctness, then compare complete elapsed CPU demand and visible response under identical workloads. Exact local candidates should retain their measured benefit without regressions in unaffected routes. Architecture candidates must also satisfy input service, simulation deadlines, bounded snapshot age and presentation coherence. Do not add percentage savings from separate builds. Re-link and remeasure every selected combination. Prefer the simplest winning architecture, remove obsolete paths, and document rejected experiments with their raw evidence.
