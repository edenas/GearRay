## Unsupported claims and research boundaries

**UNVERIFIED CLAIM:** 60 fixed simulation ticks/s; artifact-free double buffering on all GG revisions; any physical button-to-photon percentile; a faster safe replacement for the installed copy; a global worst-case stack bound. None is claimed achieved.

No useful publication establishes the exact speed of GearRay's mask/span/LUT candidates. Their support is algebra, emitted code and measured ROM execution. Manufacturer documentation, reverse engineering, engineering experience and academic scheduling theory are deliberately distinguished above. Sega transcriptions and the rolling SDCC manual have provenance/version limitations; those are not hidden by calling every reference official.

Specific source gap: S02 marks page5 missing at the VRAM-write subsection. Its readable read-access section specifies28 clocks and its control-port section describes the interrupt/reset-latch hazard. The write-loop recommendation here comes from the installed SMSlib implementation and reproduced timings, not an invented quotation from Sega's missing page.
